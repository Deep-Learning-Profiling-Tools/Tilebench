## Notes on specific cells
- 1d/2d/3d_conv tensor/ALU pipeline % are the report's family-wide ranges (0.9-15% TC, 60-74% ALU); the report gives no per-backend split, so both columns carry the range with that caveat.
- flash_attention tensor pipe 33-36% and streamk_matmul 46-51% are ranges the report gives for 'the two DSLs' jointly.
- matmul_fp32_fp16_fp8: instruction ratio NA (report gives Tensor-pipe %, tile shapes, TMA GB, not instruction counts).
- linear_self_attention: instruction ratio NA overall (report gives per-kernel factors: z 1.75x, out_gemm 3.5x, kv_gemm below Triton).
- softmax: XU cycles 1.9x is not an instruction ratio -> NA; primary mechanism 'other' = transcendental (exp) lowering, which is not in the allowed list.
- kl_divergence: primary 'other' = cuTile box-load/TMA streaming slightly higher DRAM utilization (cuTile faster by ~8%).
- sigmoid secondary 'other' = ct.exp transcendental lowering (report p27).
- histogramming occupancy = warps_active 21.6%/10.8% (report p7 screenshot); radix_sort occupancy = scatter-stage 45%/23% (report p9); cross_entropy = active-warps 27%(fp32 34%)/82% (report p36); mean_reduction given as resident threads/SM (2048 vs 768) since the report states it that way.
- matmul_int8: the report gives no instruction/register/occupancy numbers; the paper's old case-study numbers (78.8M vs 187.0M SASS instructions, 255 regs, 346.88/477.44 us) are NOT in NVIDIA_Report(2) and contradict the current CSV (0.3289 vs 0.3272 ms parity) -> treat as stale, not reproduced here.
- flash_attention: the paper's old numbers (22.77 vs 17.70 ms, 43.2% compute throughput, 185 live registers, Triton without TMA) are NOT in NVIDIA_Report(2) and contradict current CSV (20.4122 vs 18.6327 ms) and the report (both use TMA + tcgen05) -> stale.
- leaky_relu, quantize_global, vector_add: absent from the report; rows are CSV/code-only, confidence low, recommended_paper_location omit.
- csv_gap_ratio direction: value = slower/faster at the representative case; faster_backend gives the direction. Full-precision latencies copied verbatim from the manifest.
