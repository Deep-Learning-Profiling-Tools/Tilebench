### Historical caveat (resolved by the change above; kept for the record)

The previous `fig_rq3_top20` picked the case with the largest `data.tensors.infer_problem_size`, which returns
`params["n"]` whenever a key `n` exists, and broke ties by keeping the first CSV row. That put `streamk_matmul`
at `m=1024` (n=28672 for every m), `histogramming` at `num_bins=64` and `top_k_selection` at `k=16` instead of the
NCU-profiled largest cases (`m=8192`, `num_bins=4096`, `k=1024`). The NCU catalogue (`tilebench_run/ncu_catalogue.py`)
had always used the largest-product case, so Fig 4 and the NCU analysis disagreed for those operators; they now agree.

## 3. Mechanism map for all 45 operators

Conventions: "T/C" = per-operator geomean speedup ratio Triton/cuTile from Fig 2 data
(`rq12_raw.json:rq1.per_op`, autotuned; >1 = Triton faster); "sweep-max C/T" = cuTile_ms/triton_ms at the
NCU-profiled case from `05_ncu_case_manifest.csv`. Each operator appears in exactly one primary mechanism;
secondaries are noted in parentheses. Report evidence is quoted from `NVIDIA_Report(2).pdf` (page).

### A. Dynamic indexing and gather/scatter lowering (8 operators)

**Members:** weight_dequant (T/C 1.730), destindex (2.096; secondary D), 1d_conv (1.760), 2d_conv (1.561),
3d_conv (2.186), 2d_max_pooling (1.303), gaussian_blur (1.214; secondary D — gap only at fp16), radix_sort (1.875).

**Mechanism (one sentence):** when the operand tile is addressed through runtime-computed or non-affine indices,
Triton's pointer-tile + masked `tl.load`/`tl.store` carries the predicate and coalesces adjacent lanes, whereas
cuTile's `ct.gather`/`ct.scatter` materialise per-element indices, masks and shared-memory staging, producing a
much longer instruction stream (and, for scatter, per-element stores that do not shrink with byte width).

**Strongest representative cases and exact report evidence:**
* **conv family (p1–2):** "approximately 60–74% of pipeline activity is associated with ALU execution, whereas
  the Tensor-Core pipeline accounts for only about 0.9–15%"; dynamic-instruction ratio cuTile/Triton
  "approximately 2.4× for 1D, 2.3× for 2D, and 3.9× for 3D"; "In the 3D FP16 case … cuTile executes
  approximately 27.8 billion instructions compared with 7.2 billion for Triton"; NCU "attributes substantial
  long-scoreboard stalls around the cuTile MMA region"; runtime "cuTile requires 1.79× and 1.73× the Triton
  latency for FP16 and FP32 in 1D convolution, approximately 1.45× and 1.50× in 2D convolution, and 2.58× and
  1.82× in 3D convolution" (CSV manifest: 1.7875/1.7302, 1.4510/1.5010, 2.5832/1.8219 ✓). Triton FP32
  bank conflicts "approximately 54.5% for 1D, 52.0% for 2D, and 79.6% for 3D, compared with only about 3–7%
  for FP16" (matches `Figures/_data_ncu_conflict.csv` C_LSU 54.51/51.93/79.63; fp16 5.30/6.77/3.03).
* **destindex (p21–22, p28–29):** Triton "DRAM utilization 达到约 78%，有效带宽约 6.0 TB/s"; cuTile
  "Compute utilization 达到约 70–76%，但有效带宽只有约 1.4–4.4 TB/s"; gap "从 fp32 的约 1.5×，扩大到
  fp16/bf16 的约 2.5×，并在 int8 下接近 2.9×" (CSV: 1.5182 / 2.5215, 2.5227 / 2.9423 ✓); Torch
  "Compute utilization 约为 63%，有效带宽只有约 1.4 TB/s".
* **weight_dequant (p12–13, screenshots):** Torch advanced indexing "把原本很小的 scale tensor S 展开成完整的
  M×N scale matrix"; ideal fp16/bf16 traffic "≈ 420 MB" vs Torch "完整 scale 矩阵: 210 MB, 两个 M×N int64
  index 网格: 2 × 10240² × 8 bytes ≈ 1.68 GB"; cuTile "ct.gather 的 lowering 更重，会引入额外的 tile index
  handling、padding/bounds logic、shared-memory staging 和更高的 register pressure，因此 cuTile 更容易表现为
  ALU/resource-bound". Sweep-max C/T: fp16 2.7452, bf16 2.7548, fp32 1.5584. (Only qualitative NCU evidence in
  the report — no instruction counts.)
* radix_sort (p8–9): scatter stage "cuTile scatter 的动态 warp instruction 数约为 Triton 的 2.31×", occupancy
  "从约 45% 降至 23%", "两边实际 DRAM traffic 基本相同"; sweep-max C/T 2.0481. Also the ct.load-vs-gather
  ablation (count kernel 0.53→4.90 ms, scatter 2.01→3.00 ms when switching to ct.load).
* 2d_max_pooling (p14–15): "ct.gather 的 lowering 比 Triton 的 2D vectorized pointer load 更重，同时 cuTile
  的 register pressure 更高" (qualitative only); C/T 1.33–1.39.

**Do not use as primary evidence:** gaussian_blur (fp32 is parity 1.018×, only fp16 1.486× — the report
attributes it to `ct.gather` on 2-byte elements + convert overhead; no NCU counters quoted); 2d_max_pooling
(qualitative only, no counters); weight_dequant for any *numeric* NCU claim (report gives traffic arithmetic
only, no instruction/occupancy numbers).

### B. Reduction granularity and primitive availability (5 operators)

**Members:** cross_entropy (T/C 1.411), moe_topk_gating (1.960), top_k_selection (1.130; not an aligned
algorithm — primitive availability), histogramming (1.625), softmax (1.522; secondary D — transcendental
lowering).

**Mechanism:** cuTile's 128-thread CTA floor and SMEM-tree cross-warp reduction (or the absence of a
primitive such as `tl.topk`/`tl.histogram`) inflate per-row instruction and synchronisation cost for small
per-row reductions where Triton can use a single-warp register/shuffle path or a native primitive.

**Strongest cases and exact evidence:**
* **cross_entropy (p36):** "cuTile 每行约执行 780 条指令，而 Triton 只有约 226 条，约为 3.45×"; "cuTile 还额外
  产生约 20 条 shared-memory 指令"; active warps "约 82%" vs Triton "约 27%（fp32 约 34%）"; final gap
  "fp16 约 1.32×、fp32 约 1.37×" (NCU-side); CSV sweep-max C/T 1.5395 / 1.5316.
* **moe_topk_gating (p35–36):** Triton winner "一个 warp … 几乎没有 shared-memory 指令"; cuTile "把 reduction
  lower 成 shared-memory tree，产生大量 shared load/store，以及约 4 倍的全局访存指令"; gap "约 2.2×"
  (CSV 2.19/2.17/2.17); Torch "约 121–133 μs … 比 Triton 慢约 10×、比 cuTile 慢约 4.7×"; working set
  "约 5.7 MB"; "TMA 和 Tensor Core 三边均未使用".
* **histogramming (p5–7, screenshots):** both DSLs "2,097,152 个 mem_global_op_atom 请求 … smem atomic 为零";
  Torch "2.69M 条 smem atomic 指令"; "triton warps_active = 21.6% / cuTile warps_active = 10.8%";
  "Triton: 822 us / cuTile: 1427 us … 1427 / 822 ≈ 1.74×" (NCU); CSV sweep-max (num_bins=4096) C/T 1.7811.
  Also the `tl.histogram` prototype table (p8): 64 bins 163 µs (4.7× vs torch, 5.8× vs current Triton);
  4096 bins 5853 µs (9.4× slower than torch, 7.3× slower than current Triton).
* softmax (p36–37): "cuTile 消耗的 XU 周期约为 Triton 的 1.9×，而且 XU 已成为 cuTile 的主要瓶颈"; gap
  "fp16 下约 1.82×、fp32 下约 1.25×" (CSV 1.8230 / 1.2381).
* top_k_selection (p10–11): "cuTile lacks a native top-k primitive … fully unrolled bitonic sorting network";
  "When K=1024, tl.topk approaches full-sort cost, while cuTile's static compare-swap network … yielding a
  1.4–1.7× per-kernel advantage and an overall win over Triton" — at the NCU-profiled case (k=1024) CSV C/T is
  0.6323 (cuTile faster); Fig 2 per-op geomean T/C 1.130 because small-k cases favour Triton.

**Do not use as clean backend evidence:** top_k_selection (algorithms differ by design — label it a
primitive-availability case study, not a codegen gap); histogramming Torch-vs-DSL crossover (algorithmic:
smem-private histogram vs global-atomic partials).

### C. Register/SMEM placement and Tensor-Core scheduling (10 operators)

**Members:** matmul_fp32_fp16_fp8 (T/C 0.788, cuTile wins), flash_attention (0.941, cuTile wins),
block_sparse_attention (2.689), batch_normalization (1.114; direction flips with dtype), jacobi_stencil_2d
(0.866, cuTile wins), linear_self_attention (2.015), streamk_matmul (2.050; parity at large m), batched_matmul
(1.131; fp32 1.76×), mean_reduction (1.139; register pressure), flash_decode (3.377; software-pipeline depth
under 16-CTA parallelism).

**Mechanism:** with regular addressing and matched algorithms, the residual gap is decided by where each
compiler keeps tile state (registers vs SMEM), how deep its software pipeline is (Triton `num_stages` vs
cuTile runtime `while` loops without pipelining), and how large a Tensor-Core tile it can afford under the
shared-memory budget — which can favour either backend.

**Strongest cases and exact evidence:**
* **matmul_fp32_fp16_fp8 (p2–3):** tensor-pipe "cuTile sustains approximately 79–81% … compared with roughly
  52–69% for Triton"; FP32: Triton "constrained to 128×128×32, whereas cuTile's compiler-managed shared-memory
  layout sustains 256×256×64 at one CTA per SM"; "NCU measures 21.5 GB of TMA loads for the Triton FP32
  kernel versus 10.7 GB for cuTile"; "Tensor pipeline at 81% active versus Triton's 52%"; runtime "1.55×
  faster in FP32 … 1.12× faster in FP16 … 1.22× faster in FP8" (CSV: 1.5451, 1.1214, 1.2155 ✓); PyTorch
  fp16 kernel "nvjet 256×256 2-CTA pair-MMA"; fp8 torch path "torch._scaled_mm with a BF16 output".
* **flash_attention (p24–25):** Torch/cuDNN "warp-specialized … producer/consumer … 128×128 query tile";
  "Torch 的 Tensor Core pipeline 活跃率约为 79%，而两个 DSL 只有约 33%–36%"; Triton "128×64，因为 128×128 会造成
  较高的寄存器压力"; cuTile "128×128 … SMEM-centric lowering … 约 230 KB shared memory"; cuTile "通常比 Triton
  快约 9%" (CSV: 1.0955× ✓). Caveat: the report's "Torch 的约 1.4–1.6× 优势" is not what the sweep-max CSV
  shows (Torch 15.187 ms vs Triton 20.4122 ms = 1.344×; vs cuTile 18.6327 ms = 1.227×) — flag before quoting.
* **linear_self_attention (p22–23):** three-way "两个核心 GEMM 现在都确认使用了 TMA + tcgen05 Tensor Core";
  Torch two GEMMs "约 37.6 μs"; Triton z_kernel "只有 8 个 CTA … 约 115 μs"; cuTile z_kernel "约 1.75× 更多指令";
  out_gemm "约 3.5× 的指令量"; kv_gemm "runtime while K-loop 没有形成 Triton num_stages=3 那样的 software
  pipeline … 单条指令平均等待接近 50 cycles"; residual gap "约 2.2–2.5×" (CSV 2.1910; NCU 2.45×).
* **block_sparse_attention (p24):** cuTile "约 2.14× 更多 warp instructions"; "约 221 KB/CTA 的 shared-memory
  staging"; stall "约 9.5 个 warp cycles，而 Triton 约 4.8"; "cuTile 的 achieved occupancy 反而略高" (so not a
  residency problem); gap "约 3×" (CSV 3.0780); Torch flex_attention "慢约 2×" (CSV 2.0681×).
* flash_decode (p23): "cuTile 执行约 1.01M warp instructions，而 Triton 只有约 0.49M，即 2.06×"; stall
  "约 20.2 warp-cycles，Triton 约 11.2，long-scoreboard 也约为 16.7 vs 9.2"; "只有 16 个 CTA"; "约 2.06× 的指令
  膨胀 × 1.8× 的平均等待深度 ≈ 实测 3.7×" (CSV 3.7162); Torch parity "45.1 vs 47.1 μs" (CSV 45.1/47.1 ✓).
* jacobi_stencil_2d (p34): Triton "1×256（fp16/bf16 下为 1×1024）的单行窄 tile" vs cuTile "4×512"; "DRAM 利用率
  约为 60% 对 Triton 的 50%，memory throughput 约为 78% 对 63%，运行时间约为 185 μs 对 222 μs"; CSV fp32
  cuTile 1.2444× faster, fp16/bf16 1.227×.
* batch_normalization (p29–30): fp16/bf16 "Triton 通常比 cuTile 快约 1.3–1.5×" (CSV 1.6054/1.5639) vs fp32
  "cuTile … 快约 1.2×" (CSV 1.1950); mechanism "register-centric" vs "SMEM-centric" lowering (qualitative).
* streamk_matmul (p34–35): Torch "约 0.92M 条 UTCMMA … 两个 DSL 需要约 3.6M 条"; TC utilisation "DSL 的约
  46%–51% 提高到接近 69%"; cuTile first_wave "小 shape 时该 kernel 可比 Triton 慢约 4×"; box-load splits
  "fp16 每步约 3 条、fp32 可达到 5 条，而 Triton 保持 2 条"; large-shape CSV C/T 1.06/1.04/1.04.
* batched_matmul (p17–18): fp32 cuTile "255 regs/thread + ~180KB smem/block … 接近 1 block/SM" (CSV fp32
  1.7637); fp16/bf16 "tile shape 和 tuner 粒度导致的小幅波动" (CSV 1.085/1.093).
* mean_reduction (p14): cuTile "fp16/fp32 大约 74–78 regs/thread，而 Triton 只有约 30 regs/thread … 从 Triton 的
  约 2048 threads/SM 降到 cuTile 的约 768 threads/SM"; bf16 anomaly "寄存器数反而较低、occupancy 更高，但带宽仍明显低".

**Do not use as clean backend evidence:** flash_attention Torch gap magnitude (report 1.4–1.6× vs CSV 1.34×/1.23×,
see caveat); streamk_matmul Fig 4 bars (small-shape, see §1 caveat); batched_matmul fp16/bf16 (tuner-granularity
noise per report); mean_reduction bf16 (report itself calls it an unexplained conversion-path anomaly);
matmul_int8 (parity → E).

### D. Dtype-dependent exposure of fixed overhead (9 operators)

**Members:** sigmoid (T/C 1.307), swiglu (1.229), relu (1.060; int8 only), mul2 (1.047; int8 only),
dequantize_rowwise (1.155; int8-only op), rope (1.142; sweep-max ≈ parity), layernorm (1.209; fp16/bf16 only),
l2_norm (1.146; fp16/bf16 only), rmsnorm (1.073).

**Mechanism:** T ≈ max(T_memory, T_issue) — narrowing the dtype halves memory time while cuTile's per-tile
bookkeeping, predicate handling, per-element conversion and special-function expansion cost stays roughly
per-element, so instruction overhead that is hidden at fp32 surfaces at fp16/bf16 and int8 (report p26–29,
both language versions).

**Strongest cases and exact evidence:**
* **sigmoid (p20–21):** Torch "有效吞吐只有约 4.45 TB/s，低于 Triton 的 5.84 TB/s"; cuTile "128 threads/CTA，而
  Triton 使用 256 threads/CTA … cuTile 比 Triton 慢约 1.5×，甚至略慢于 Torch"; fp32 "60.8/60.9/61.0 μs" (CSV
  60.8/60.9/61.0 ✓); CSV fp16/bf16 C/T 1.4722/1.4906, fp32 1.0016.
* **swiglu (p25–26):** Triton fp16 "DRAM utilization 约为 87.5%，有效带宽约 6.71 TB/s"; cuTile "DRAM utilization
  只有约 66.9%，有效带宽约 5.13 TB/s，比 Triton 慢约 1.3–1.4×"; fp32 "收敛到约 1.0×" (CSV 1.3280/1.2994/1.0201).
* **relu (p37):** int8 "DRAM 利用率下降到约 35%–44% … cuTile 的总动态指令数约为 Triton 的 1.85× … 约 1.34× 的
  Triton/cuTile 差距" (CSV int8 1.3370); fp32 "有效带宽约 5.6 TB/s … Triton/cuTile 基本为 1.0" (CSV 1.0073).
* mul2 (p27–28): "在 INT8 下约为 1.15×" (CSV int8 1.1379; fp32 1.0073).

  大约两倍的 load 指令"; "cuTile 的动态指令数约为 Triton 的 2.1×"; "约 1.19× 的单 kernel 延迟差" (CSV 1.2073).
* layernorm (p26), l2_norm (p31–32), rmsnorm (p19–20): qualitative fp16/bf16 vs fp32 statements only
  (l2_norm "Triton 通常领先约 1.2–1.3×" — CSV 1.2848/1.3038, fp32 1.0263; rmsnorm "cuTile 通常比 Triton 慢约 3% 到
  10%" — CSV 1.1746/1.1323/1.0241; layernorm no numbers — CSV 1.6047/1.5785/0.9812).

**Do not use as primary evidence:** layernorm (no NCU numbers in report; and its fp16 gap 1.60× is larger than the

also explicitly names the control group for this mechanism: "纯 matrix_copy 和结构对称的 matrix_transpose … 几乎没有
表现出 dtype 相关的差距" (p27/29).

### E. Backend parity and control cases (13 operators)

**Members:** matrix_copy (T/C 0.989), matrix_transpose (1.003), quantize_global (1.005), interleave (1.018),
reverse_array (1.009), matmul_int8 (1.018 — parity in the final data), vector_add (1.009), leaky_relu (0.997),
dropout (1.006), fused_activation (1.010), bitonic_sort (0.996), argmax (0.966), kl_divergence (0.957).

**Mechanism:** when the kernel is deeply memory-bound with regular affine access (or both backends hit the same
expressibility wall), tile-lowering overhead is hidden behind DRAM waits and the two DSLs land within measurement
noise of each other; these operators calibrate what "no backend gap" looks like.

**Exact evidence:**
* fused_activation (p32–33): "两边都达到约 84%–85% DRAM utilization … Triton/cuTile 长期接近 1.0"; Torch traffic
  "672 MB" vs fused "336 MB … 理论流量比正好是 2×" (CSV T/torch 2.000).
* dropout (p32): Torch "约 210 MB" vs "约 126 MB，理论流量比 210/126 ≈ 1.67×" (CSV 1.616/1.650/1.608);
  "DRAM 利用率和运行时间几乎一致".
* interleave (p30–31), reverse_array (p33): "没有显著 DSL gap，当前基本 parity"; reverse_array "CSV 中个别 1.05–1.1× 的
  波动主要来自不同配置选择和测量噪声" (CSV 1.059/1.130/0.989/0.984).
* bitonic_sort (p32): "端到端时间几乎一致 … 约 300 次串行的 L2 往返"; both DSLs "缺少能够在 tile 内表达任意 lane
  permutation 的机制" (CSV 0.977/1.017; 301 launches each).
* matmul_int8 (p18–19): Triton "legacy IMMA.16832 … 不是 tcgen05"; cuTile "UTCIMMA … tcgen05"; "Triton 和 cuTile 现在
  性能几乎打平 … 剩余瓶颈不在 MMA 指令代际本身" (CSV 1.0052); Torch is an "exact fp32 emulation baseline" (~10×).
* argmax (p16–17), kl_divergence (p33–34): cuTile slightly faster at fp32 (CSV 0.893 / 0.921); kl_divergence
  "约为 79% 和 6.0 TB/s，而 Triton 约为 74.5% 和 5.7 TB/s"; both "深度 memory-bound".
* matrix_copy / matrix_transpose (p26–29): named control cases; CSV C/T 0.99–1.02 (copy), 0.98–1.00 (transpose).
* quantize_global, leaky_relu, vector_add: **not covered in NVIDIA_Report(2)**; parity claim rests on CSV only
  (C/T 1.000; 1.003/1.006/1.013; 0.996/1.005/1.000/1.024).

**Do not use as backend-gap evidence:** any of the above; matmul_int8 must not be described as a cuTile win (the
old paper text 346.88 vs 477.44 µs / 1.38× is superseded — CSV 0.3289 vs 0.3272 ms); bitonic_sort Torch gap
is a hand-written-baseline artefact per report ("数十倍落后并不能代表 PyTorch 排序能力").
