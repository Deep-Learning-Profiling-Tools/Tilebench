## 3. Claims in the source NOT confirmed (or contradicted) by introspection

- "`T.annotate_safe_value({x: -T.infinity(dtype)})` fixes the `T.copy` zero-fill" (source §3, §8): the annotation and the pass exist, but `copy.cc` emits its own `make_zero` fill and `LegalizeSafeMemoryAccess` runs after `LowerTileOp`; whether the annotation overrides the copy's fill was not established without compiling. Stated conservatively in the Reference.
- "The slice `end` in `x[.., start:end]` is largely dead code" (source §3): contradicted by `copy_op._normalize_copy_regions` (`BufferRegion -> [r.extent]`); not carried over.
- "fp16/bf16 math is native (`hexp`/`__habs` in codegen)" (source §6): the device-side definitions exist in `common.h`; the codegen path that selects them was not traced. Stated as "native device implementations exist".
- "`T.Kernel(...)` yields `[bx, by, bz]` in x,y,z order": the list order was inferred from `frames[0:-4]` iteration in `KernelLaunchFrame.__enter__`; x-first ordering matches the docstring example `as (bx, by)` but was not executed.
- `T.annotate_padding` (mentioned in no section but probed): does not exist in 0.1.11.
- Timing/size statements ("~seconds per recompile", "~3.4 s on B200", "safe inside a Proton timing window"): out of scope, not verified, removed.
- `tilelang.autotune` internals (direct-JIT bypass rule, `kernel.config`, `update_tuner_result`): symbol existence confirmed only; semantics deliberately not re-verified because the section was removed.
